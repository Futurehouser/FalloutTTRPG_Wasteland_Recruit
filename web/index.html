Wasteland Recruit

Your Fallout 2d20 companion

Hold and drag a tile to move it (click and drag with a mouse).

Random Character

One tap rolls a full level-1 character and fills in the character sheet. Tap the sheet to zoom.

Your filled character sheet will appear here.

Scavenging Location

Rolls a ready-to-run location: search test, loot ranges, inhabitants, obstacles and hazards. Tap a loot category to open it in the Loot Roller.

Loot Roller

Pick a category, enter your 2d20 total (2–40) and get the item and how many dice to roll for the quantity. The default tables are this app's own. Use Edit this table to type in the tables from your rulebook.

Edit this table

Qty = fixed amount, Dice = number of combat dice (d6) added to the quantity. Changes are saved on this device.

Random Fallout 76 Perk

Picks a random perk card from the fallout.wiki Fallout 76 Perks page. Needs internet the first time.

Create Character

Fill in your character and save it. It will appear under Saved Characters.

Skills (rank 0–6, tick = Tag skill)

Fallout 76 perk cards (used for perk suggestions)

Encounter Randomizer

When the party rests or travels, roll 2d20 and pick the party's level. You get an encounter that fits them: enemies are only chosen if they suit that level, and their numbers scale with the party's size.

Combat Assistant

Set up you, your weapon and the enemies, then the app walks you through every attack: to-hit roll, hit location, damage, effects and HP for everyone. Type in your own dice, or tap Roll for me. The fight is saved as you go.
